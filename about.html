About Aakash & Associates

A firm built by people who used to sit where you sit.

We started as a two-partner audit practice in 2001 with one belief: clients deserve advisors who understand their business, not just their balance sheet. Twenty-five years on, that's still the whole strategy.

Our story

From a two-desk office to a 12-country network

Aakash & Associates was founded on the idea that rigorous, independent assurance shouldn't require a client to become a small fish in a very large pond. We've grown by referral and reputation — not acquisition — which means every office we open still carries the same standard the founders set on day one.

Today we serve mid-market and enterprise clients across banking, manufacturing, technology, healthcare and the public sector, backed by a global network of independent member firms that lets us follow our clients wherever they grow.

Our values

What doesn't change as we grow

Independence, without exception

We turn down engagements that create conflicts, even lucrative ones. Our opinion is only worth something if it's genuinely ours.

Partner-led, always

A partner scopes your engagement, stays on the file, and signs the opinion. No hand-offs to whoever's free that week.

Plain language, real answers

If we can't explain a finding to your board in two minutes, we haven't finished the analysis. We keep going until we can.

Technology in service of judgment

We use data analytics and automation to do the sampling faster — so our people spend their time on the questions that actually matter.

Quality is a system, not a promise

Every file passes through structured technical review before it leaves the building. No exceptions for deadline pressure.

Long-term over billable

We'd rather tell you something you don't want to hear this year than something you didn't need to hear for five more.

Milestones

Two decades of growing with our clients

2001 — Founded

Opened as a two-partner statutory audit practice serving local manufacturing and trading businesses.

2008 — Tax & Advisory launch

Added dedicated tax and business advisory practices as clients began cross-border expansion.

2013 — Risk Consulting practice

Built an internal audit and enterprise risk practice in response to new governance regulation.

2018 — Global network membership

Joined an international network of independent firms, extending our reach to 12 countries.

2022 — Audit analytics platform

Rolled out proprietary data-analytics tooling across every audit engagement, firm-wide.

2026 — 500+ clients, 180+ professionals

Marking 25 years with our largest team yet and the same partner-led model we started with.
