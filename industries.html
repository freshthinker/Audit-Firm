Industries

Sector expertise that speaks your language

Regulation, unit economics and risk look different in every industry. Our teams specialize so you never have to explain the basics of your business twice.

Banking & Financial Services

Regulatory reporting, prudential audit and risk-model validation for banks, NBFCs and asset managers navigating tightening oversight.

Manufacturing

Inventory, cost-audit and supply-chain controls testing for manufacturers managing thin margins and complex working capital.

Technology & SaaS

Revenue recognition, R&D capitalization and SOC-readiness support for software companies scaling toward their next raise.

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Compliance-heavy audit and reimbursement-cycle reviews for hospital groups, diagnostics chains and healthtech providers.

Real Estate & Construction

Percentage-of-completion revenue testing and project-finance audits for developers and construction groups.

Retail & Consumer

Inventory shrinkage analytics and omni-channel revenue audits for retail and D2C brands scaling across geographies.

Energy & Utilities

Asset-heavy audit, regulatory tariff reviews and ESG-disclosure assurance for energy and infrastructure operators.

Government & Public Sector

Grant utilization audits, public-fund assurance and performance reviews for government bodies and NGOs.

Why sector focus matters

Generalist auditors miss industry-specific risk

A revenue recognition question in SaaS is not the same question in construction. Our teams are organized around industries first, service lines second — so the person testing your controls has actually seen your business model before.
