What we do

Four practices. One accountable team.

Whichever service brought you here, the same partner-led team follows your engagement from scoping to sign-off — no silos, no hand-offs.

01 · Audit & Assurance

Independent audits your stakeholders actually trust

Statutory and voluntary audits, reviews and agreed-upon procedures — delivered with data-driven sampling and a technical review process that catches issues before your board does.

  • Statutory & group audits (standalone and consolidated)
  • IFRS / Ind AS / US GAAP conversion support
  • Internal financial controls (IFC) testing
  • Special-purpose & regulatory audits
Request an audit proposal

02 · Tax Advisory

Tax strategy that scales with cross-border ambition

From annual compliance to holding-structure design, we help you keep more value in the business without stepping anywhere near a grey area.

  • Direct & indirect tax compliance and filings
  • Transfer pricing documentation & benchmarking
  • Cross-border structuring & treaty planning
  • Tax dispute resolution & representation
Talk to a tax advisor

03 · Risk & Consulting

Governance that becomes a competitive advantage

We build internal audit and risk functions that regulators respect and management actually uses — not a binder that sits on a shelf until the next inspection.

  • Internal audit co-sourcing & outsourcing
  • Enterprise risk management (ERM) design
  • Fraud investigation & forensic accounting
  • IT & cybersecurity risk assessments
Assess our risk practice

04 · Transaction Advisory

Deal conviction, backed by real diligence

Buy-side and sell-side due diligence, valuations and post-merger integration support for deals that need to close on time and hold up under scrutiny.

  • Financial & commercial due diligence
  • Business & share valuations
  • Deal structuring & SPA support
  • Post-merger integration & carve-outs
Discuss a transaction
